FEATURES / YOUR LIBRARY

More than
turning pages.

Discover Zhimor's tools for managing, reading, importing and protecting your library.

01 / FEATURE

Your library and multiple formats

Import and organise your own files, shelves, groups and covers. Includes TXT, EPUB, MOBI, AZW, AZW3, AZW4, PRC, PDB, uPDB, PDF, DOCX, RTF, Markdown, CBZ, CBR and related archives. Files may be parsed, converted or extracted rather than rendered natively.

02 / FEATURE

Reading appearance and tools

Customise fonts, text size, spacing, themes, page turns and scrolling. Search text, save bookmarks, notes and highlights, and use text replacement or dictionary rules where supported.

03 / FEATURE

Comics, PDFs and media

Dedicated reading views for comics and PDFs; separate modules handle supported audio and video files. Controls and compatibility vary by file.

04 / FEATURE

Reading progress and insights

Review reading time, progress, completion, trends, statistics by book and reading goals.

05 / FEATURE

Ways to bring in books

Import through Files, the share extension or local Wi-Fi transfer. Optionally connect WebDAV, FTP, SFTP, SMB, OPDS, Baidu Netdisk and Aliyun Drive. Availability depends on the service, authorisation and network.

06 / FEATURE

iCloud, backup and listening

Optional iCloud data synchronisation, separate local backup and restore tools, and text-to-speech with supported voice or network services.
