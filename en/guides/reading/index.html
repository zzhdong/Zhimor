GUIDE / READING

Reading layouts and chapter headings

Choose text direction, two-column settings and heading styles for your books.

← All guides

01

Two-column reading

Turn on dual columns in reading settings and adjust the column gap, divider style, width, height and opacity. Narrow body width and some scrolling or listening modes temporarily fall back to a single page.

02

Vertical writing

Select Vertical Layout in the reader. Text flows top-to-bottom in right-to-left columns, with matching page progression. This is text orientation, not portrait device orientation. Support varies by publication and mode.

03

Design custom chapter headings

Open the chapter heading centre, choose a built-in style or create one. Arrange elements in the visual designer, choose fonts, or edit HTML/CSS templates and preview light/dark appearances. Where applicable, preserve the publisher's headings.

04

Move styles and troubleshoot

Custom heading styles can be imported or exported. If a layout does not appear, check file type, the active setting, scrolling or listening state and the available text width.
