GUIDE / SYNC

iCloud synchronisation

Optionally sync supported reading data between devices.

← All guides

01

Before enabling

Sign in with the appropriate Apple account, confirm iCloud availability and check sync status in Zhimor. Network conditions and storage limits may affect operation.

02

What may sync

Depending on your settings, metadata, groups, progress, bookmarks, notes, insights, covers and reading settings may sync through Apple's iCloud services. Reading files only sync when supported and selected.

03

Allow time to complete

Initial upload and cross-device merging can take time, especially with large collections. Keep a working network connection and avoid repeatedly deleting or reimporting the same items.

04

Before turning it off

Disabling sync does not necessarily delete data already in iCloud. It is not a substitute for a separate backup; manage cloud-stored content through Apple where appropriate.

Still need help?

Include your device, OS, app version and steps to reproduce. Never send passwords, tokens or private books.
