GUIDE / NETWORK

Network libraries and drives

Connect storage services and files you are authorised to use.

← All guides

01

Choose a connection type

Open My Cloud Disk and select WebDAV, SMB, SFTP, FTP or OPDS as appropriate. Enter the server address and credentials provided by your service. Available operations vary by protocol.

02

Connect a cloud provider

Baidu Netdisk and Aliyun Drive use their own authorisation flows. No separate Zhimor account is required for local reading. Provider availability may change.

03

Troubleshoot connections

Check the host, port, network, certificate and credentials, and verify the server permits access. Prefer encrypted connections on trusted networks; FTP or HTTP can offer weaker transport protection.

04

Respect content rights

Zhimor does not include or guarantee third-party books. Connect only files and services you have permission to access.

Still need help?

Include your device, OS, app version and steps to reproduce. Never send passwords, tokens or private books.
