SIGNATURE READING

A reading layout that suits you.

Two-column pages, vertical text and custom chapter headings are purpose-built reading options.

01 / DOUBLE COLUMN

Two-column reading

Split body text into two columns and customise the gutter and divider. Falls back to single page when the layout is unsuitable.

02 / VERTICAL TEXT

Vertical writing

Read top-to-bottom text in right-to-left columns with matching page direction, where the format and reading mode support it.

03 / CHAPTER DESIGN

Custom chapter headings

Choose or design heading styles, preview fonts and layouts, edit HTML/CSS and import or export styles.
