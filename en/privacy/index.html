Privacy Policy

How Zhimor stores information, uses optional network services and lets you manage your data.

This page is generated from the privacy agreement bundled with the Zhimor app. Last updated: 8 October 2026.

Zhimor is a reading and library-management app built around data minimisation and user control. The app contains no advertising, does not track users across apps or websites, does not use IDFA and does not build developer-side advertising profiles from reading habits. A Zhimor account is not required to use the app.

1. Data on your device

To operate the local library and reader, the app may store imported files, book metadata, covers, library groups, reading progress, bookmarks, notes, reading statistics, display and read-aloud settings, file-processing caches and necessary diagnostic information.

App statistics are used primarily for on-device viewing and diagnostics and are not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated analytics service.

A stable installation identifier may be stored in Keychain to support local data consistency, diagnostics and data-write identification. It is not an advertising identifier, an Apple ID or a device serial number.

2. Optional iCloud synchronisation

When an iCloud account is available and synchronisation is enabled, Zhimor may use Apple's CloudKit, iCloud Documents or iCloud key-value storage to synchronise book metadata, library groups, reading progress, bookmarks, notes, reading statistics, covers, reading settings and selected reading files.

Such data is kept in the user's iCloud storage, subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. Disabling synchronisation does not necessarily remove content already uploaded to iCloud.

3. Network access initiated by you

If you choose to use cloud-file access, web browsing, online read-aloud or other optional network features, the app may send necessary requests, file information, account credentials or connection details to the selected service. That service may process these under its own policy.

Such requests do not mean Zhimor sends reading records or credentials to its own analytics backend. Enable network features as needed and review the privacy policies of the services you use.

4. System permissions

Zhimor requests permissions when needed for the related feature, such as camera access for scanning, Photos access for selecting or saving images, and biometrics for on-device privacy protection. Declining optional permissions will not prevent unrelated reading features from working. You may change permissions in system settings.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete books and related records, clear supported caches and change sync settings in the app, or uninstall the app and its local data using the operating system. Some data retained by system services or backups may need to be managed separately.

Use Apple's storage-management tools for data already synchronised to iCloud. Manage data and authorisations held by other services through those services.

6. Contact and policy changes

Zhimor does not automatically upload local reading records or diagnostic logs to a developer analytics service. If you email support, the email provider and recipient receive the information you choose to send.

Material changes to data handling will be reflected in the policy and renewed consent sought where required by law.
