Frequently asked questions

How do I import books?

Use the import option in Zhimor or share/open a supported file from Apple's Files app. Compatibility depends on the format and the contents of each file.

Why does a cloud provider ask me to sign in?

Services such as Baidu Netdisk, Aliyun Drive and network storage are optional. Authorisation is handled by the selected service. Zhimor does not require its own account for local reading.

How do I troubleshoot iCloud syncing?

Check that devices are using the same Apple Account, iCloud is available, network connectivity works, and your app sync/backup settings are correct. Disabling sync does not necessarily delete existing iCloud data.

How can I manage permissions or delete data?

You can change camera or Photos permissions in device settings. Use the app's management features to remove locally stored books and related records, and Apple's iCloud storage tools to manage synchronised content.

What should I include in a report?

Please provide your device model, OS and Zhimor version, reproduction steps and redacted screenshots where helpful. Never email passwords, access tokens or private book contents.
