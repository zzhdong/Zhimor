Frequently asked questions

How do I import books?

Use import in your bookshelf, share from Files or use local Wi-Fi transfer.

Why won't a file open?

File integrity, encoding, encryption and format conversion can affect compatibility. Try an intact, unencrypted common-format file.

How do I use two-column, vertical text or custom chapter headings?

Find layout settings in the reader and open the chapter-heading centre for custom styles. Two-column mode may fall back on narrow pages or during scrolling/listening. See the reading guide for details.

Why does a storage service ask me to sign in?

Optional third-party services may require their own login. Local Zhimor reading does not require a Zhimor account.

Why hasn't iCloud data appeared?

Check the Apple account, sync settings, network and storage. Initial synchronisation and merges can take time.

How can I protect and restore data?

Export a separate rbak backup and review its restore preview. Disabling sync usually does not automatically erase iCloud data.
